A single 1024×1024 front page summing up world news from **Wednesday 25 November 2026** — every story on it happened or was first reported on that day, and no story comes from another day.

- 3 hero stories: US tariffs on 14 nations, the first Pakistani astronaut docking at Tiangong, and the Kospi's 6.2% plunge.
- A dotted world map with 13 numbered pins marking where each story happened.
- 10 flag cards, a UTC timeline of the day, and a numbered list of all 29 stories.
- A DATE CHECK footer: nothing on the page is from another day.

`main.py` is standard library only — it copies the pre-rendered `entry.png` into the grader's output directory.
